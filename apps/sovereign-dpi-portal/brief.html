What you are looking at

One web page, four sovereign applications, one shared truth. A citizen sends money in the wallet; the revenue authority sees the treasury credit; the central bank sees the message and the reserves — in the same second, with the same reference. Nothing is copy-pasted between screens: every number on every tab is produced by one settlement event. And the counterparty throughout is the Bank of Zambia itself — this is central bank infrastructure, not a local-bank product.

APP 1

Bank of Zambia Observer

The central bank’s own supervisory node — deliberately the first tab you see. Every digital Kwacha in circulation is shown against its reserve held at the Bank of Zambia itself — 1:1, at all times, no commercial-bank escrow — plus the raw ISO 20022 message stream, schema-validated on every message.

LOOK FOR — the solvency gauge holding 100.00% through mint/burn events, and the message inspector (pacs.008 / pain.001 / pacs.002) carrying the ZRA split inside the payment itself.
APP 2

ZRA Treasury & Customs

The Treasury Single Account as a live ledger. Every cross-border settlement streams in with the automated 0.50% sovereign cut already split off and cleared — no forms, no reconciliation, no leak.

LOOK FOR — the settlement stream ticking every few seconds; the TSA balance animating upward on each credit; “TSA CLEARED” on every row.
APP 3

GovPay Citizen & Merchant Wallet

The citizen and trader experience — a Kwacha wallet that pays across the border at mobile-money speed, priced at 1.5% total instead of 7–10% bank wires. Familiar rails on the surface (MTN MoMo · Airtel Money · card); sovereign settlement underneath.

TRY THIS — type an amount, watch the ZRA/GovPay/agent split recompute live, press SEND: a settlement receipt with finality, reference hash, QR and bank-grade ISO 20022 message.
APP 4

INRIS Ghost-Buster SCT

Social cash transfer the way BoI/MCDSS wish it worked: every beneficiary verified against the national registry before payment. Duplicates — the ghost payroll that drains social programs — are blocked at source.

TRY THIS — press RUN SCT BATCH (2,000 wallets): payments stream in green “PAID” and duplicate NRCs are stopped in red, while the savings counter ticks up.
The one thing to watch: send money in App 3 (the wallet), then switch to App 2 — the same transaction reference appears as the highlighted top row of the ZRA stream, with its +K treasury credit. Switch back to App 1 — the same payment arrives as a schema-validated ISO 20022 message. Revenue, proof and oversight move together because they are one event, not three systems.

The pitch in one paragraph

Zambia's problem is not money — it is leakage, delay and proof. Money crosses borders at 7–10% and takes days; social programs pay ghosts; the treasury sees revenue only after costly reconciliation. This suite shows all three fixed by one infrastructure: every payment carries its treasury split, its registry check and its central-bank visibility inside the payment itself — settled in well under one second (54–400ms in our live tests), presented to citizens through the mobile-money rails they already trust.
