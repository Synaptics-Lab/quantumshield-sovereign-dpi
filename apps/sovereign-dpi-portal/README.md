# GovPay Sovereign DPI Suite

> **Category:** Sovereign Digital Public Infrastructure (DPI)  
> **Jurisdiction Pilot:** Republic of Zambia (Smart Zambia Institute & Bank of Zambia Architecture)

## Overview
The GovPay Sovereign DPI Suite demonstrates a complete national digital public infrastructure built on high-performance Layer-1 distributed ledger technology. It provides institutional rails for revenue collection, social cash transfers, central bank digital reserves, and real-time gross settlement (RTGS).

## Core Modules
1. **Zambia Revenue Authority (ZRA) Automated Tax-Split:**
   - Automatically diverts a 0.50% statutory levy on all merchant transactions directly to the Single Treasury Account (TSA) with zero intermediary handling.
2. **Bank of Zambia (BoZ) 150M ZMW Reserve Vault:**
   - Verifiable on-chain reserve backing the national digital currency (ZMW).
3. **INRIS Biometric Soulbound Identity (SBT):**
   - Cryptographic identity verification preventing "ghost employee" payroll duplication in government disbursements.
4. **ISO 20022 Pacs.008 Payment Router:**
   - Commercial bank interbank messaging natively compiled on-chain.
